What We're Studying

A Deep Dive Into the Gift of the Holy Spirit

This season, our Bible Study Series is exploring what it means that God didn't just save us and leave us on our own — He gave us the Holy Spirit to guide, comfort, convict, and empower us. Together we're digging into scripture, real-life application, and honest conversation about what it looks like to actually walk in the Spirit, day to day.

This is a safe, honest, and welcoming space for women to grow in the Word together — no perfect answers required, just an open heart.

Group Details

  • Meets twice a month, running now through December
  • Group discussion, scripture study, and real-life application
  • Open to Christ and Coffee members and regular attendees

For everyone's safety and privacy, meeting times and locations aren't posted publicly. Email us below and a Christ and Coffee leader will get you connected with the group.
