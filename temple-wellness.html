What It Is

Faithful Stewardship, Not Another Diet

Temple Wellness is a 6-week faith-based wellness program for women covering physical health, mental health, and nutrition — because stewarding our bodies is an act of worship. It isn't about chasing perfection or fitting into a smaller size. It's about practical habits, movement, nutrition, and accountability, held together by biblical truth and real community.

🍎

Nutrition

Practical, whole-food-focused guidance — flexible enough for whatever approach you've chosen, whether that's keto, intuitive eating, or anything in between.

🧠

Mental Health

Honest conversations about the mind-body connection, stress, and rest — because wellness isn't just physical.

🙏

Spiritual Grounding

Every habit is anchored in scripture and identity in Christ — never in a number on a scale.
